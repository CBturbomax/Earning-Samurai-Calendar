About Community Financial System, Inc.

 

Community Financial System, Inc. is a diversified financial services company that is focused on four main business lines – banking services, employee benefit services, insurance services and wealth management services. Its banking subsidiary, Community Bank, N.A., is among the country’s 100 largest banking institutions with over $17 billion in assets and operates approximately 200 customer facilities across Upstate New York, Northeastern Pennsylvania, Vermont, Western Massachusetts and Southern New Hampshire. The Company’s Benefit Plans Administrative Services, Inc. subsidiary is a leading provider of employee benefits administration, trust services, collective investment fund administration, and actuarial consulting services to customers on a national scale. The Company’s OneGroup NY, Inc. subsidiary is a top 63 U.S. insurance agency. The Company also offers comprehensive financial planning, trust administration and wealth management services through its Nottingham Financial Group operating unit. The Company is listed on the New York Stock Exchange and the Company’s stock trades under the symbol CBU. For more information about the Company and each of its four main business lines visit https://ir.cfsi.com.

Community Financial System, Inc. Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results

“Our businesses accelerated into the third quarter and we delivered operating diluted earnings per share1 of $1.27, up 16.5% year-over-year and 9.5% quarter-over-quarter, representing our sixth consecutive quarter of record results,” commented Dimitar A. Karaivanov, President and CEO.

 

“During the quarter we achieved an operating return on assets1 of 1.51% while retaining excellent balance sheet metrics. In addition, we decided to reposition the investment portfolio and pull forward some of our securities cash flows in order to both capture positive economic value and provide increased visibility into the embedded repricing earnings power of our balance sheet. Our trajectory remains intact and we expect strong performance across all of our businesses into the fourth quarter and 2027.”

Results of Operations

 

The Company reported third quarter 2026 net income of $61.3 million, or $1.16 per share. This compares to net income of $55.1 million, or $1.04 per share, for the third quarter of 2025. The $0.12 increase in earnings per share was primarily driven by increases in net interest income and noninterest revenues, partially offset by increases in noninterest expenses and income taxes. Comparatively, the Company’s earnings per share was consistent with the linked second quarter of 2026 as an increase in net interest income was offset by a realized loss on investment securities along with increases in the provision for credit losses and noninterest expenses.

 

 

Net Interest Income and Net Interest Margin

 

The Company’s record quarterly net interest income reflected lower funding costs, organic loan growth and strategic balance sheet actions, including the redeployment of proceeds from the sale of lower-yielding U.S. Treasury securities to repay higher-cost borrowings, which supported continued margin expansion.

 

·Net interest income in the third quarter of 2026 was $143.5 million, up $15.4 million, or 12.0%, compared to the third quarter of 2025, and up $4.4 million, or 3.2%, from the second quarter of 2026.

·Net interest margin for the third quarter of 3.54% and fully tax-equivalent net interest margin, a non-GAAP measure, of 3.56%, increased by 24 basis points and 23 basis points, respectively, from the third quarter of 2025. These increases were primarily the result of a lower cost of interest-bearing liabilities and a higher yield on interest-earning assets.

·The yield on interest-earning assets increased 8 basis points to 4.67% over the prior year’s third quarter primarily driven by an increase in the proportion of loan balances to total interest-earning assets supported by organic loan growth and the sale of $296.8 million of lower-yielding available-for-sale U.S. Treasury securities.

·The cost of interest-bearing liabilities decreased 21 basis points from 1.76% in the third quarter of 2025 to 1.55% in the third quarter of 2026, primarily driven by a 19 basis point decrease in the average rate paid on interest-bearing deposits.

·On a linked quarter basis, net interest margin and fully tax-equivalent net interest margin, a non-GAAP measure, increased by 8 basis points and 7 basis points, respectively. The yield on interest-earning assets increased 6 basis points, while the cost of funds decreased 2 basis points. This included a 1 basis point decrease in the cost of interest-bearing liabilities driven by a 4 basis point decrease in the average interest-bearing deposit rate to 1.40%. Excluding the impact of the semiannual Federal Reserve Bank dividend recorded in the second quarter of 2026, the yield on interest-earning assets increased 8 basis points compared to the linked second quarter.

 

Noninterest Revenues

 

The Company’s noninterest revenue streams (excluding losses on investment securities) generated 38% of total revenues in the third quarter.

 

·Banking noninterest revenues, comprised of deposit service and other banking fees, totaled $22.5 million for the third quarter of 2026, an increase of $1.3 million, or 6.2%, from the third quarter of 2025, and an increase of $1.2 million, or 5.5%, from the second quarter of 2026. The increase from the third quarter of 2025 was primarily comprised of higher debit interchange and ATM fees. The increase from the linked second quarter was due in part to higher customer interest rate swap fee revenues.

·Employee benefit services revenues for the third quarter of 2026 were $36.4 million, an increase of $2.0 million, or 5.8%, in comparison to the third quarter of 2025 and an increase of $1.5 million, or 4.4%, from the second quarter of 2026. The increases between both periods were largely driven by revenue growth in the recordkeeping and third-party administration services business line due in part to higher average market values of assets under administration.

·Insurance services revenues for the third quarter of 2026 were $14.6 million, an increase of $0.5 million, or 3.4%, versus the prior year’s third quarter and an increase of $1.4 million, or 10.7%, from the second quarter of 2026. The increase from the prior year’s third quarter was driven by revenue growth from acquisitions, while the increase from the linked second quarter reflected the seasonal timing of insurance policy renewals.

·Wealth management services revenues for the third quarter of 2026 totaled $12.4 million, an increase of $3.4 million, or 38.4%, from the third quarter of 2025, and an increase of $2.0 million, or 19.0%, from the second quarter of 2026. The increases between both periods were reflective of revenue growth from the acquisition of ClearPoint Federal Bank & Trust (“ClearPoint”) and higher average market values of assets under management.

·The Company recognized a $2.9 million net loss on investment securities during the third quarter of 2026 which included a $3.0 million realized loss on the sale of $296.8 million of lower-yielding available-for-sale U.S. Treasury securities. The proceeds from the sale were used to repay higher-cost overnight borrowings, which was immediately accretive to net interest income.

Noninterest Expenses and Income Taxes

 

The Company continues to focus on managing expenses consistent with its organic growth strategies and scale objectives, while evaluating efficiency opportunities and the enhancement of operating leverage in all lines of business.

 

·The Company recorded $141.0 million in total noninterest expenses in the third quarter of 2026, compared to $128.3 million of total noninterest expenses in the prior year’s third quarter. The $12.7 million, or 9.9%, increase between the periods was primarily driven by higher salaries and employee benefits expenses, data processing and communications expenses and occupancy and equipment expenses.

·Operating noninterest expenses, a non-GAAP measure, were $136.5 million in the third quarter of 2026, an increase of $12.2 million, or 9.8%, compared to the third quarter of 2025.

·Salaries and employee benefits expenses increased $7.4 million, or 9.7%, from the third quarter of 2025, primarily due to incremental costs associated with acquisitions completed between the periods, higher performance-based incentive compensation, increased employee medical costs and annual merit-based salary increases.

·Data processing and communications expenses increased $2.5 million, or 13.0%, from the third quarter of 2025 reflective of the Company’s continued investment in customer-facing and back-office technologies, including artificial intelligence applications and other workflow efficiency initiatives. The increase also reflected a one-time $0.8 million consulting expense related to the renegotiation of a debit card branding contract, which is expected to generate future cost savings.

·Occupancy and equipment expenses increased $2.6 million, or 22.9%, from the prior year’s third quarter, driven by incremental costs associated with the opening of de novo bank branches and regional headquarters and the Santander Bank, N.A. (“Santander”) branch acquisition.

·The effective tax rate for the third quarter of 2026 was 24.0%, a decrease from 24.7% in the third quarter of 2025 and 24.1% in the second quarter of 2026. The effective tax rate for the first nine months of 2026 was 23.8% compared to 23.3% for the first nine months of 2025 driven by an increase in certain state income taxes.

 

Financial Position and Liquidity

 

The Company’s financial position and liquidity profile remain strong, demonstrating the effectiveness of its proactive asset and liability management and prudent financial planning.

 

·The Company’s total assets were $17.79 billion at September 30, 2026, representing an $830.2 million, or 4.9%, increase from one year prior and a $24.2 million, or 0.1%, increase from the end of the second quarter of 2026. The increase in the Company’s total assets from one year prior was primarily driven by organic loan growth supported by the funding generated from the Santander branch acquisition and the ClearPoint acquisition.

·At September 30, 2026, the Company’s readily available sources of liquidity totaled $6.52 billion, including unrestricted cash and cash equivalents balances of $419.9 million, unpledged investment securities totaling $1.52 billion, unused borrowing capacity at the Federal Home Loan Bank of New York of $1.62 billion and $2.96 billion of funding availability at the Federal Reserve Bank’s discount window.

·The Company’s readily available sources of liquidity represent 229% of the Company’s estimated uninsured deposits, net of collateralized and intercompany deposits, at September 30, 2026.

·Estimated insured deposits, net of collateralized and intercompany deposits, represented 81% of total ending deposits at September 30, 2026.

Deposits and Funding

 

The Company continues to leverage its strong core deposit base, characterized by low funding costs, to support its financial operations.

 

·Ending deposits at September 30, 2026 of $14.87 billion were $154.7 million, or 1.1%, higher than the end of the second quarter of 2026 and were $808.3 million, or 5.7%, higher than one year prior. The increase from June 30, 2026 was primarily due to seasonal inflows of governmental deposit balances while the increase from one year prior was primarily driven by growth in consumer and business deposit balances, including the $543.7 million of deposits assumed in the Santander branch acquisition and the $120.1 million of deposits assumed in the ClearPoint acquisition. Excluding the deposits assumed in acquisitions, ending deposits increased $144.5 million, or 1.0%, from one year prior.

·Ending borrowings of $636.0 million at September 30, 2026, which consisted of $412.9 million of fixed rate Federal Home Loan Bank of New York term borrowings, $215.0 million of customer repurchase agreements and $8.1 million of finance lease liabilities, decreased $128.1 million, or 16.8%, from the end of the second quarter of 2026 and decreased $127.3 million, or 16.7%, from one year prior. The decrease between both periods primarily reflected a decrease in overnight borrowings following the Company’s redeployment of cash from the sale of $296.8 million of lower-yielding available-for-sale U.S. Treasury securities.

·The Company’s average cost of funds of 1.16% decreased 17 basis points from the third quarter of 2025 and decreased 2 basis points from the second quarter of 2026. The decreases between both periods reflected lower average deposit costs and a lower proportion of funding from higher rate borrowings.

·The quarterly average cost of total deposits of 1.03% remains comparatively low relative to the industry and decreased 14 basis points from the third quarter of 2025 and 4 basis points from the linked second quarter of 2026.

·66% of the Company’s total deposits were in no- and relatively low-rate checking and savings accounts at the end of the third quarter of 2026. Time deposit accounts represented 13% of the Company’s total deposits at the end of the third quarter of 2026, a decrease of 1 percentage point from September 30, 2025 and the end of the linked second quarter.

 

Loans and Credit Quality

 

The Company’s predominantly footprint-based loan portfolio is well diversified, with credit performance remaining a central priority. The Company’s asset quality metrics, including net charge-offs and delinquent and nonperforming (nonaccrual loans and accruing loans 90 days or more past due) loan levels, remain strong compared to the banking industry, reflecting the Company’s robust risk management practices and disciplined credit quality standards.

 

·Ending loans at September 30, 2026 totaled $11.52 billion, an increase of $235.2 million, or 2.1%, compared to June 30, 2026 and an increase of $767.8 million, or 7.1%, compared to one year prior. The increases between both periods reflected organic growth in the overall business and consumer lending portfolios. The Company’s non-owner occupied and multifamily CRE exposure remains diverse both geographically and by property type, and relatively low at 16% of total assets, 25% of total loans and 202% of total bank-level regulatory capital.

·At September 30, 2026, the Company’s allowance for credit losses totaled $92.0 million, or 0.80% of total loans outstanding, compared to $91.7 million, or 0.81% of total loans outstanding, at June 30, 2026, and $84.9 million, or 0.79% of total loans outstanding, at September 30, 2025. The increase from one year prior was driven by a net reserve build in the business lending portfolio reflective of organic loan growth, including growth in larger commercial lending relationships and higher qualitative reserve factors related to those exposures, along with a moderate increase in delinquent loan balances.

·The Company recorded a $5.6 million provision for credit losses during the third quarter of 2026 compared to $4.6 million in the linked second quarter and $5.6 million in the prior year’s third quarter.

·The Company recorded net charge-offs of $5.1 million, or an annualized 0.18% of average loans, in the third quarter of 2026 compared to net charge-offs of $2.5 million, or an annualized 0.09% of average loans, in the third quarter of 2025 and net charge-offs of $3.3 million, or an annualized 0.12% of average loans, in the second quarter of 2026. The increase in net charge-offs compared to both periods was primarily driven by $2.7 million of charge-offs recognized in the third quarter of 2026 on 2 individually assessed business lending relationships for which specific allowance allocations had previously been established.

·Total delinquent loans, consisting of loans 30 or more days past due and nonaccrual loans, as a percentage of total loans outstanding was 1.16% at the end of the third quarter of 2026. This compares to 1.04% at June 30, 2026 and 1.00% at September 30, 2025. The increases between both periods were primarily driven by higher delinquent balances of business lending and consumer mortgage loans.

·At September 30, 2026, nonperforming loans were $60.4 million, or 0.52% of total loans outstanding, compared to $56.8 million, or 0.50% of total loans outstanding at June 30, 2026, and $56.1 million, or 0.52% of total loans outstanding, one year earlier.

Shareholders’ Equity and Regulatory Capital

 

The Company’s capital planning and management activities, coupled with its diversified streams of income and prudent dividend practices, have allowed it to build and maintain a strong capital position. At September 30, 2026, all of the regulatory capital ratios of the Company and Community Bank, N.A. (“CBNA”) significantly exceeded well-capitalized standards.

 

·Shareholders’ equity of $2.03 billion at September 30, 2026 was $91.6 million, or 4.7%, higher than one year ago, primarily due to a $134.6 million increase in retained earnings partially offset by a $34.2 million increase in accumulated other comprehensive loss related to the Company’s investment securities portfolio. Shareholders’ equity decreased $42.2 million, or 2.0%, from June 30, 2026, primarily driven by a $61.6 million increase in accumulated other comprehensive loss related to the Company’s investment securities portfolio, partially offset by a $35.9 million increase in retained earnings.

·The Company’s shareholders’ equity to assets ratio was 11.42% at September 30, 2026, a decrease from 11.43% at September 30, 2025 and 11.67% at June 30, 2026.

·The Company’s tier 1 leverage ratio of 9.37% at September 30, 2026 remained substantially above the regulatory well-capitalized standard of 5.0% and decreased 9 basis points from one year earlier and increased 11 basis points from June 30, 2026. The decrease in the Company’s tier 1 leverage ratio from one year prior was primarily due to the intangible assets added as part of the Santander branch and ClearPoint acquisitions, as well as the impact of $31.8 million of common stock repurchases over the past twelve months.

·The Company’s tangible equity to tangible assets ratio (non-GAAP) was 6.57% at September 30, 2026, down from 6.73% a year earlier and 6.86% at June 30, 2026. Tangible equity (non-GAAP) increased $25.6 million, or 2.4%, from one year prior due to the aforementioned increase in retained earnings that was partially offset by an increase in accumulated other comprehensive loss related to the Company’s investment securities portfolio and the intangible assets added as part of the Santander branch and ClearPoint acquisitions. Tangible assets (non-GAAP) increased $764.2 million, or 4.7%, from the prior year due primarily to organic loan growth, supported by the funding generated from the Santander branch acquisition and the ClearPoint acquisition.

 

Dividend Increase and Stock Repurchase Program

 

The payment of a meaningful and growing dividend is an important component of the Company’s commitment to provide consistent and favorable long-term returns to its shareholders, and it reflects the continued strength of the Company’s long-term operating results and capital position, and management’s confidence in the future performance of the Company. The $0.02 increase in the quarterly dividend declared in the third quarter of 2026 marked the 34th consecutive year of dividend increases for the Company.

 

·During the third quarter of 2026, the Company declared a quarterly cash dividend of $0.49 per share on its common stock, up 4.3% from the $0.47 dividend declared in the second quarter of 2026 and the third quarter of 2025.

Non-GAAP Measures

 

The Company also provides supplemental reporting of its results on an “operating” and “tangible” basis. Results on an “operating” basis exclude the after-tax effects of acquisition expenses, acquisition-related contingent consideration adjustments, restructuring expenses, litigation accrual, gain (loss) on investment securities and amortization of intangible assets. Results on a “tangible” basis exclude goodwill and intangible asset balances, net of accumulated amortization and applicable deferred tax amounts. The Company also provides supplemental ratio reporting at the segment level, which includes adjusted return on tangible equity. Adjusted return on tangible equity represents annualized adjusted income before income taxes applicable to each segment as a percentage of average tangible equity for each respective segment. In addition, the Company provides supplemental reporting for “operating pre-tax, pre-provision net revenues,” which adds back the provision for credit losses, acquisition expenses, acquisition-related contingent consideration adjustments, restructuring expenses, litigation accrual, gain (loss) on investment securities and amortization of intangible assets from income before income taxes. Although these items are non-GAAP measures, the Company’s management believes this information helps investors and analysts measure underlying core performance and provides better comparability to other organizations that have not engaged in acquisitions. The Company also provides supplemental reporting of its net interest income and net interest margin on a fully tax-equivalent (“FTE”) basis, which includes an adjustment to net interest income that represents taxes that would have been paid had nontaxable investment securities and loans been taxable. Although FTE net interest income and net interest margin are non-GAAP measures, the Company’s management believes this information helps enhance comparability of the performance of assets that have different tax liabilities. The amounts for such items are presented in the tables that accompany this release.
