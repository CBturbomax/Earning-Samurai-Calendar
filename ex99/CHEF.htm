The Chefs’ Warehouse Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
Ridgefield, CT, October 28, 2026 - The Chefs’ Warehouse, Inc. (NASDAQ: CHEF) (the “Company” or “Chefs’”), a premier distributor of specialty food products in the United States, the Middle East, and Canada, today reported financial results for its third quarter ended September 25, 2026.

Financial highlights for the third quarter of 2026:

•Net sales increased 11.2% to $1.14 billion for the third quarter of 2026 from $1.02 billion for the third quarter of 2025.
•GAAP net income was $29.6 million, or $0.67 per diluted share, for the third quarter of 2026 compared to $19.1 million, or $0.44 per diluted share, in the third quarter of 2025.
•Adjusted net income per share1 was $0.66 for the third quarter of 2026 compared to $0.50 for the third quarter of 2025.
•Adjusted EBITDA1 was $82.2 million for the third quarter of 2026 compared to $65.1 million for the third quarter of 2025.

“Revenue trends displayed typical seasonal cadence through the third quarter of 2026, building from the summer months into September with strong year-over-year growth. Our regional Chefs’ Warehouse teams delivered excellent execution across markets and product categories – driving strong growth in revenue and gross profit dollars; as well as continued progress increasing relevance with our customer base with solid year-over-year growth in unique item placements and unique customers. Our Middle East operations continue to operate effectively in a challenging environment. Revenue trends in recent weeks have been approximately 100% of prior-year, as food-away-from-home activity is supported by local residents and ex-patriots returning from the summer off-season”, said Christopher Pappas, Chairman and Chief Executive of the Company. “Momentum continued into October, and as we head into the busy holiday season, I would like to thank all our Chefs’ Warehouse teams, from sales, procurement and operations to all the supporting functions, for their dedication and commitment to delivering our diverse, high-quality product, and service, in partnership with our suppliers and customers and the communities we serve.”

Third Quarter Fiscal 2026 Results

Net sales for the third quarter of 2026 increased 11.2% to $1.14 billion from $1.02 billion in the third quarter of 2025. Organic sales increased $105.4 million, or 10.3% versus the prior year quarter. Sales growth of $8.7 million, or 0.9%, was primarily a result of acquisitions. Organic case count increased approximately 6.1% in the Company’s specialty category for the third quarter of 2026 with unique customer and placement increases of 4.4% and 7.2% respectively, compared to the third quarter of 2025. Organic pounds sold in the Company’s center-of-the-plate category increased approximately 6.6% for the third quarter of 2026 compared to the prior year quarter.

Gross profit increased 15.0% to $284.3 million for the third quarter of 2026 from $247.2 million for the third quarter of 2025. The increase in gross profit dollars was primarily a result of increased sales volumes, price inflation and acquisitions. Gross profit margins increased approximately 84 basis points to 25.0%. Gross profit margins increased 63 basis points in the Company’s specialty category and increased 137 basis points in the center-of-the-plate category.

Selling, general and administrative expenses increased by approximately 12.1% to $233.4 million for the third quarter of 2026 from $208.1 million for the third quarter of 2025. The increase was primarily due to higher costs associated with compensation and benefits, facilities and distribution to support sales growth, fuel costs, as well as higher depreciation expense driven by facility and fleet investments. 2026 Guidance

We are raising our fiscal 2026 full year financial guidance as follows:

•Net sales in the range of $4.56 billion to $4.61 billion,
•Gross profit to be between $1.130 billion and $1.140 billion and
•Adjusted EBITDA1 to be between $317 million and $321 million.

About The Chefs’ Warehouse

The Chefs’ Warehouse, Inc. (http://www.chefswarehouse.com) is a premier distributor of specialty food products in the United States, the Middle East and Canada focused on serving the specific needs of chefs who own and/or operate some of the nation’s leading menu-driven independent restaurants, fine dining establishments, country clubs, hotels, caterers, culinary schools, bakeries, patisseries, chocolateries, cruise lines, casinos and specialty food stores. The Chefs’ Warehouse, Inc. carries and distributes more than 90,000 products to more than 55,000 customer locations throughout the United States, the Middle East and Canada.
