C.H. Robinson Reports 2026 Third Quarter Results
Eden Prairie, MN, October 28, 2026 - C.H. Robinson Worldwide, Inc. (“C.H. Robinson”) (Nasdaq: CHRW) today reported financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Third Quarter Highlights:
•Company has continued to deliver secular earnings growth driven by consistent market share gains, disciplined revenue management and evergreen productivity improvements fueled by its Lean AI strategy
•North American Surface Transportation ("NAST") volume increased approximately 3.5% year-over-year compared to a 0.3% decline in the Cass Freight Shipment Index, reflecting the 14th consecutive quarter of market outgrowth
•NAST truckload adjusted gross profit ("AGP") per shipment(1) increased 17.0% year-over-year, despite a significant increase in truckload spot market costs, due to disciplined revenue management
•NAST and Global Forwarding both achieved their mid-cycle operating margin targets
•Income from operations increased 23.1% to $271.8 million
•Adjusted income from operations(1) increased 26.1% to $290.8 million
•Diluted earnings per share ("EPS") increased 24.6% to $1.67
•Adjusted diluted EPS(1) increased 28.6% to $1.80
•Cash generated by operations decreased by $151.3 million to $124.1 million due to unfavorable changes in working capital driven by higher transportation rates
•Cash returned to shareholders increased 52.5% to $289.1 million
(1) Adjusted gross profit, adjusted income from operations, and adjusted diluted EPS are non-GAAP financial measures. The same factors described in this release that impacted these non-GAAP measures also impacted the comparable GAAP measures. Refer to pages 11 through 14 for further discussion and GAAP to Non-GAAP Reconciliations.

Summary of Third Quarter of 2026 Results Compared to the Third Quarter of 2025
•Total revenues increased 29.5% to $5.4 billion, primarily driven by higher pricing in our truckload, ocean, less than truckload ("LTL") and air services and higher volume in our truckload services.
•Gross profits increased 15.0% to $795.6 million. Adjusted gross profits(1) increased 14.5% to $808.4 million, primarily driven by higher adjusted gross profit per transaction in our truckload, ocean, air and LTL services and higher volume in our truckload and LTL services.
•Operating expenses increased 10.6% to $536.6 million. Personnel expenses increased 3.1% to $360.3 million, primarily due to higher incentive compensation reflecting our strong operating performance. This was partially offset by cost optimization efforts, productivity improvements and lower restructuring charges. Average employee headcount declined 10.4%. Other selling, general and administrative (“SG&A”) expenses increased 29.7% to $176.3 million, primarily due to higher claims expenses and an impairment charge on our Kansas City regional center lease driven by the execution of a sublease agreement for the remaining portion of the facility.
•Income from operations totaled $271.8 million, up 23.1% due to the increase in adjusted gross profit, partially offset by the increase in operating expenses. Adjusted operating margin(1) of 33.6% increased 230 basis points.
•Interest and other income/expense, net totaled $21.7 million of expense, consisting primarily of $20.4 million of interest expense, which increased $4.7 million versus last year due to a higher average debt balance, partially offset by lower variable interest rates. The third quarter of 2026 results also include a $2.1 million net loss from foreign currency revaluation and realized foreign currency gains and losses.
•The effective tax rate in the quarter was 20.6%, which was flat compared to the third quarter of 2025.
•Net income totaled $198.5 million, up 21.8% from a year ago. Diluted EPS of $1.67 increased 24.6%. Adjusted diluted EPS(1) of $1.80 increased 28.6%.

Third quarter total revenues for the NAST segment totaled $3.8 billion, an increase of 29.1% over the prior year, primarily driven by higher pricing and volume in our truckload and LTL services. NAST adjusted gross profits increased 16.6% in the quarter to $517.9 million. Adjusted gross profits in truckload increased 20.7% due to a 17.0% increase in adjusted gross profit per shipment and a 3.5% increase in volume. Our average truckload linehaul rate per mile charged to our customers, which excludes fuel surcharges, increased approximately 31.5% in the quarter compared to the prior year, while truckload linehaul cost per mile, excluding fuel surcharges, increased 33.5%, resulting in a 21.5% increase in truckload adjusted gross profit per mile. LTL adjusted gross profits increased 8.0% versus the year-ago period, driven by a 4.0% increase in adjusted gross profit per order and a 3.5% increase in LTL volume. Total NAST truckload and LTL volume increased 3.5% versus the year-ago period and outpaced the market indices for the 14th consecutive quarter. Operating expenses increased 15.7%, primarily due to higher claims expenses and higher incentive compensation reflecting our strong operating performance, partially offset by cost optimization efforts and productivity improvements. Third quarter average employee headcount was down 11.4% year-over-year. Income from operations increased 17.9% to $203.9 million, and adjusted operating margin expanded 50 basis points to 39.4%. Adjusted operating margin - excluding specified items(1) increased 110 basis points to 40.3%.

Cash Flow Generation and Capital Distribution
Cash generated from operations totaled $124.1 million in the third quarter, compared to $275.4 million in the third quarter of 2025. The $151.3 million decrease in cash flow from operations was primarily related to a $246.4 million decrease in cash generated by changes in net operating working capital, due to a $267.4 million sequential increase in net operating working capital in the third quarter of 2026 compared to a $21.0 million sequential increase in the third quarter of 2025. This was partially offset by a $35.5 million increase in net income.
In the third quarter of 2026, cash returned to shareholders totaled $289.1 million, with $214.3 million in repurchases of common stock and $74.8 million in cash dividends.
Capital expenditures totaled $17.5 million in the quarter. Capital expenditures for 2026 are expected to be $65 million to $75 million.
