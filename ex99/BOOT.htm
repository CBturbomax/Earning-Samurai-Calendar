Boot Barn Holdings, Inc. Announces Second Quarter Fiscal Year 2027 Financial Results and Authorizes an Additional $400 Million Under Its Share Repurchase Program

IRVINE, California – October 28, 2026 – Boot Barn Holdings, Inc. (NYSE: BOOT) (the “Company,” “Boot Barn,” “we,” “us,” and “our”) today announced its financial results for the second fiscal quarter ended September 26, 2026. A Supplemental Financial Presentation is available at investor.bootbarn.com.

For the quarter ended September 26, 2026 compared to the quarter ended September 27, 2025:

●Net sales increased 15.1% to $581.8 million.
●Same store sales increased 1.4%, with retail store same store sales increasing 1.0% and e-commerce same store sales increasing 5.2%.
●Merchandise margin rate increased by 190 basis points, including a 130 basis-point benefit from lower freight expense, a 40 basis-point benefit from tariff refunds, and 20 basis points of product margin expansion.
●Earnings per diluted share increased 26.3% to $1.73, compared to $1.37 per diluted share in the prior-year period. Included in earnings per diluted share is an estimated $0.05 per share benefit from tariff refunds. Excluding the impact of tariff refunds, earnings per diluted share increased 22.6%.
●The Company opened 19 new stores, bringing its total store count to 584 as of the quarter end.

John Hazen, Chief Executive Officer, commented, “We are pleased with our second quarter results, as continued revenue growth and merchandise margin expansion contributed to a 26% increase in earnings per diluted share. Our new stores continue to exceed expectations, with recent openings generating sales above our original projections. This performance reflects the growing national reach of the Boot Barn brand and reinforces our confidence in the company's long-term expansion opportunity.”

Mr. Hazen continued, “Looking ahead, we are excited about the holiday season and the work our teams have done to prepare, including refreshed merchandise assortments, improved in-store presentations, and a significantly expanded national marketing campaign. Together with the continued momentum in our work business and the relaunch of Boot Barn Workforce, these initiatives provide additional opportunities to drive sales and support our long-term growth.”

Share Repurchase Program

Under its $200 million share repurchase program (the “Repurchase Program”), the Company repurchased 240,937 and 399,388 shares of its common stock for an aggregate purchase price of $35 million and $60 million during the thirteen and twenty-six weeks ended September 26, 2026, respectively.

On October 21, 2026, the Company’s Board of Directors authorized the Company to repurchase an additional $400 million of shares of its common stock under the Company’s existing repurchase program. Following this increase, approximately $468 million remained available for future share repurchases under the current repurchase program. Except with respect to the increase in the authorized share repurchase amount, the repurchase program otherwise remains unchanged.

Operating Results for the Second Quarter Ended September 26, 2026 Compared to the Second Quarter Ended September 27, 2025

●Net sales increased 15.1% to $581.8 million from $505.4 million in the prior-year period. Consolidated same store sales increased 1.4%, with retail store same store sales increasing 1.0% and e-commerce same store sales increasing 5.2%. The increase in net sales was the result of incremental sales from new stores and the increase in consolidated same store sales.
●Gross profit was $216.5 million, or 37.2% of net sales, compared to $184.1 million, or 36.4% of net sales, in the prior-year period. Included in gross profit is $2.2 million of tariff refunds recognized in cost of goods sold during the current-year period. The remaining increase was driven by an increase in sales, partially offset by the occupancy costs of new stores. The 80 basis-point increase in gross profit rate was driven primarily by a 190 basis-point increase in merchandise margin rate partially offset by 110 basis points of deleverage in buying, occupancy and distribution center costs. The 190 basis-point increase in merchandise margin rate was primarily driven by a 130 basis-point benefit from lower freight expense in the current year, combined with elevated freight costs in the prior-year period, a 40 basis-point benefit from tariff refunds recognized during the current-year period and 20 basis points of product margin expansion.
●Selling, general and administrative (“SG&A”) expenses were $146.2 million, or 25.1% of net sales, compared to $127.7 million, or 25.3% of net sales, in the prior-year period. The increase in SG&A expenses compared to the prior-year period was primarily the result of higher store payroll and store-related expenses associated with operating more stores, corporate general and administrative expenses, and marketing expenses in the current-year period. SG&A expenses as a percentage of net sales leveraged by 10 basis points primarily as a result of lower corporate general and administrative expenses in the current-year period.
●Income from operations increased $13.8 million to $70.2 million, or 12.1% of net sales, compared to $56.4 million, or 11.2% of net sales, in the prior-year period, primarily due to the factors noted above.
●Income tax expense was $18.2 million for the thirteen weeks ended September 26, 2026 compared to $14.7 million for the thirteen weeks ended September 27, 2025. Our effective tax rate was 25.8% for each of the thirteen weeks ended September 26, 2026 and September 27, 2025.
●Net income was $52.5 million, or $1.73 per diluted share, compared to $42.2 million, or $1.37 per diluted share, in the prior-year period. The increase in net income was primarily attributable to the factors noted above.

Operating Results for the Six Months Ended September 26, 2026 Compared to the Six Months Ended September 27, 2025

●Net sales increased 16.4% to $1.175 billion from $1.009 billion in the prior-year period. Consolidated same store sales increased 3.0%, with retail store same store sales increasing 2.4% and e-commerce same store sales increasing 9.2%. The increase in net sales was the result of incremental sales from new stores and the increase in consolidated same store sales.
●Gross profit was $456.4 million, or 38.8% of net sales, compared to $381.4 million, or 37.8% of net sales, in the prior-year period. Included in gross profit is $16.9 million of tariff refunds recognized in cost of goods sold during the current-year period. The remaining increase was driven by an increase in sales, partially offset by the occupancy costs of new stores. The 100 basis-point increase in gross profit rate was driven primarily by a 200 basis-point increase in merchandise margin rate partially offset by 100 basis points of deleverage in buying, occupancy and distribution center costs. The 200 basis-point increase in merchandise margin rate was driven by a 140 basis-point benefit from tariff refunds recognized during the current-year period, 40 basis points of product margin expansion, and a 20 basis-point benefit due to lower freight expense.

●SG&A expenses were $295.6 million, or 25.2% of net sales, compared to $254.2 million, or 25.2% of net sales, in the prior-year period. The increase in SG&A expenses compared to the prior-year period was primarily the result of higher store payroll and store-related expenses associated with operating more stores, corporate general and administrative expenses, and marketing expenses in the current-year period. SG&A expenses as a percentage of net sales was flat compared to the prior-year period.
●Income from operations increased $33.6 million to $160.8 million, or 13.7% of net sales, compared to $127.1 million, or 12.6% of net sales, in the prior-year period, primarily due to the factors noted above.
●Income tax expense was $40.5 million, or a 24.8% effective tax rate, compared to $32.6 million, or a 25.4% effective tax rate, in the prior-year period. The decrease in the effective tax rate was primarily due to a higher income tax benefit from income tax accounting for stock-based compensation in the current-year period.
●Net income was $122.6 million, or $4.02 per diluted share, compared to $95.6 million, or $3.11 per diluted share, in the prior-year period. Included in net income per diluted share is an estimated $0.43 per share benefit from tariff refunds. The increase in net income was primarily attributable to the factors noted above.

Fiscal Year 2027 Outlook

The Company is providing updated guidance for the fiscal year ending March 27, 2027, which supersedes in its entirety the previous guidance issued in its first fiscal quarter earnings report on July 29, 2026. For the fiscal year ending March 27, 2027, the Company now expects:

●To open 75-80 stores.
●Total sales of $2.565 billion to $2.593 billion, representing growth of 14% to 15% over Fiscal 2026.
●Consolidated same store sales growth of 0.8% to 2.0%, with retail store same store sales growth of 0.5% to 1.7% and e-commerce same store sales growth of 3.0% to 4.0%.
●Merchandise margin between $1.343 billion and $1.357 billion, or approximately 52.3% of sales, representing growth of 140 basis points over Fiscal 2026. Included in merchandise margin is an estimated $17.6 million benefit related to tariff refunds.
●Gross profit between $985 million and $999 million, or approximately 38.4% to 38.5% of sales.
●SG&A expenses between $634 million and $637 million, or approximately 24.7% to 24.6% of sales.
●Income from operations between $351 million and $362 million, or approximately 13.7% to 14.0% of sales.
●Net income of $263.6 million to $272.0 million.
●Net income per diluted share of $8.72 to $9.00 based on 30.2 million weighted average diluted shares outstanding, representing growth of 19% to 22% over Fiscal 2026. Included in net income per diluted share is an estimated $0.45 benefit related to tariff refunds.
●Effective tax rate of 25.7% for the remaining six months of the fiscal year.
●Capital expenditures between $135 million and $140 million, which is net of estimated landlord tenant allowances of $54.4 million.

For the third fiscal quarter ending December 26, 2026, the Company expects:

​

●Total sales of $785 million to $802 million, representing growth of 11% to 14% over the prior-year period.
●Consolidated same store sales declines of (1.0)% to growth of 1.0%, with retail store same store sales declines of (1.0)% to growth of 1.0%, and e-commerce same store sales declines of (1.0)% to growth of 1.0%.
●Merchandise margin between $404 million and $413 million, or approximately 51.5% of sales. Included in merchandise margin is an estimated $0.7 million benefit related to tariff refunds.
●Gross profit between $311 million and $319 million, or approximately 39.6% to 39.8% of sales.
●SG&A expenses between $187 million and $189 million, or approximately 23.9% to 23.6% of sales.
●Income from operations between $123 million and $130 million, or approximately 15.7% to 16.2% of sales.
●Net income per diluted share of $3.03 to $3.19, based on 30.0 million weighted average diluted shares outstanding.
