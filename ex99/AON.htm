Aon Reports Third-Quarter 2026 Results

DUBLIN - October 28, 2026 - Aon plc (NYSE: AON) today reported results for the three months ended September 30, 2026.
•Aon delivered another quarter of strong performance, including 3% total revenue growth, 6% organic revenue growth and EPS growth. Our results showcased broad-based strength across solution lines, with organic revenue growth of 7% in Commercial Risk, 7% in Reinsurance, 6% in Health and 4% in Wealth
•Our disciplined capital allocation framework is supported by strong free cash flow generation. We expect the USI Acquisition to close in the fourth quarter of 2026, and will prioritize debt repayment with a path towards deleveraging to our 2.8x-3.0x objective in approximately 24 months
•We enter the fourth quarter with significant momentum, reinforcing our confidence in our ability to deliver strong financial performance for full-year 2026

THIRD-QUARTER 2026 FINANCIAL SUMMARY

Total revenue in the third quarter increased 3% to $4.1 billion compared to the prior-year period, reflecting 6% organic revenue growth, partially offset by a 3% unfavorable impact primarily from divestitures. Risk Capital revenue increased $157 million, or 6%, to $2.7 billion and Human Capital revenue decreased $54 million, or 4%, to $1.4 billion.

Total operating expenses in the third quarter increased 3% to $3.3 billion compared to the prior-year period due primarily to an increase in expense associated with 6% organic revenue growth, investments in long-term growth, and an increase in Accelerating Aon United Program expenses, partially offset by lower expenses associated with the sale of the NFP Wealth business and $20 million of net restructuring savings. Risk Capital operating expenses increased $134 million, or 7%, and Human Capital operating expenses decreased $68 million, or 6%.

Foreign currency translation had a $0.02 favorable impact on both diluted EPS and adjusted EPS in the third quarter. If currency were to remain stable at today’s rates, the Company would expect a de minimis impact on adjusted EPS in the fourth quarter of 2026 and a favorable impact on adjusted EPS of approximately $0.42 per share for the full year 2026.

Effective tax rate was 20.6% in the third quarter compared to 21.3% in the prior-year period. After adjusting to exclude the applicable tax impact associated with certain non-GAAP adjustments, the adjusted effective tax rate for the third quarter of 2026 was 20.2% compared to 19.2% in the prior-year period. The primary drivers of the change in the effective tax rate were changes to the geographical distribution of income and a greater net unfavorable impact from discrete items. The primary drivers of the change in the adjusted effective tax rate were changes to the geographical distribution of income and a net unfavorable impact from discrete items.

Weighted average diluted shares outstanding decreased to 213.0 million in the third quarter compared to 216.7 million in the prior-year period. The Company did not repurchase any shares in the third quarter. As of September 30, 2026, the Company had approximately $7.7 billion of remaining authorization under its share repurchase program.

YEAR-TO-DATE 2026 CASH FLOW SUMMARY

Cash flows provided by operations for the first nine months of 2026 increased $93 million, or 4%, to $2.2 billion compared to the prior-year period, as strong adjusted operating income growth offset the impact of working capital and USI-related transaction costs.

Total revenue increased $104 million, or 3%, compared to the prior-year period, reflecting 6% organic revenue growth, driven by net new business and ongoing strong retention, partially offset by a 3% unfavorable impact primarily from divestitures largely due to the sales of the NFP Wealth business and Stroz Friedberg. Risk Capital revenue increased $157 million, or 6%, and Human Capital revenue decreased $54 million, or 4%.

Risk Capital

Commercial Risk Solutions Organic revenue growth of 7% reflects strong growth in North America and EMEA, driven by net new business and ongoing strong retention. Net market impact was slightly positive. Within North America, performance was highlighted by strong growth in M&A services and U.S. core P&C, and growth in construction.

Reinsurance Solutions Organic revenue growth of 7% reflects strong growth in treaty placements, driven by net new business and strong retention, and a favorable impact from the reversal of the constraint applied against prior period revenue. Net market impact was unfavorable in the quarter.

Human Capital

Health Solutions Organic revenue growth of 6% reflects strong growth in core health and benefits, including particular strength internationally, driven by net new business and ongoing strong retention, as well as growth in Talent Solutions driven by talent analytics. Net market impact was modestly positive.

Wealth Solutions Organic revenue growth of 4% reflects strong growth in Retirement, driven by continued demand for advisory work in the UK and EMEA related to the ongoing impact of regulatory change.
