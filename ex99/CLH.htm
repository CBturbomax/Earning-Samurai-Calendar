Clean Harbors Announces Third-Quarter 2026 Financial Results

•Delivers 21% Growth in Revenues to a Record $1.87 Billion, Driven by Both Operating Segments
•Generates 75% Increase in Q3 Net Income to $208.1 Million, or EPS of $3.93
•Achieves 42% Growth in Q3 Adjusted EBITDA to $456.1 Million; Expands Adjusted EBITDA Margin YoY by 370 Basis Points to 24.4%
•Raises 2026 Guidance for Both Adjusted EBITDA and Adjusted Free Cash Flow
•Announces Executive Transition – Eric Gerstenberg to Become Sole CEO on January 1; Mike Battles to Retire as Co-CEO and Board Member at Year-End (See Separate News Release Issued Today)

NORWELL, Mass. – October 28, 2026 – Clean Harbors, Inc. (“Clean Harbors” or the “Company”) (NYSE: CLH), the leading provider of environmental and industrial services throughout North America, today announced financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
“Our third-quarter results reflect the strength of our market position and robust demand for our environmental services and sustainable products,” said Eric Gerstenberg, Co-Chief Executive Officer. “Revenues increased 21% and Adjusted EBITDA grew 42% year-over-year, resulting in a record Adjusted EBITDA margin of 24.4%. Environmental Services (ES) delivered better-than-expected results, supported by a sizeable emergency response event, growth in PFAS-related work, strong waste volumes and pricing initiatives designed to offset inflation and higher fuel costs. Safety-Kleen Sustainability Solutions (SKSS) also generated significant profitability, as market pricing for our re-refined products remained high amid global shortages. Together, these results drove record adjusted free cash flow of $323 million. At the same time, we continued to execute safely, with our year-to-date Total Recordable Incident Rate of 0.49 on track to match the best performance in our history.”
Third-Quarter 2026 Results
Revenues increased 21% to $1.87 billion, compared with $1.55 billion in the same period of 2025. Income from operations rose 65% to $318.1 million, compared with $193.0 million in the third quarter of 2025.
Net income increased 75% to $208.1 million, or $3.93 per diluted share, compared with $118.8 million, or $2.21 per diluted share, for the same period in 2025.
Adjusted EBITDA (see description and reconciliation below) increased 42% to $456.1 million from $320.2 million for the same period in 2025.

Third-Quarter 2026 Segment Review
“Our ES segment generated an Adjusted EBITDA margin of 27.4%, marking its 18th consecutive quarter of year-over-year margin expansion,” said Gerstenberg. “Our Field Services business was the largest contributor with revenue growth of more than 70%. This was driven by considerable emergency response activity, including one substantial event that generated approximately $120 million of revenue at emergency response rates, given the scale and expedited timeframe of the work. Revenue in Technical Services rose 3% with incineration utilization of 87%. Underlying disposal and recycling demand remained healthy, with deferred revenue increasing 20% due to our backlog of waste and growing sales pipeline. Safety-Kleen Environmental Services revenue rose 11%, supported by pricing and continued momentum in its core offerings. Industrial Services posted modest growth despite ongoing softness in refinery turnaround activity. Overall, ES delivered another exceptional quarter, underscoring the broad-based demand trends we have seen throughout 2026.”
“SKSS segment revenue increased 46% in Q3 and Adjusted EBITDA was 209% higher, with segment margin more than doubling from the year-ago period,” said Mike Battles, Co-Chief Executive Officer. “This segment continues to benefit from market dynamics, with global supply disruptions supporting higher prices for refined products, including base oil and lubricants. We also continued to improve our mix and profitability, increasing Group III production by approximately 20% from a year ago and selling more direct blended gallons. Importantly, we also maintained discipline around feedstock costs, collecting 62 million gallons of waste oil while maintaining an average charge-for-oil position. The combination of strategic execution and favorable market conditions drove the highest quarterly revenue and profitability in SKSS history.”
Business Outlook
“We continue to pursue our growth strategy through a combination of acquisitions and organic growth investments,” Gerstenberg said. “The successful completion of the ES&H and EnviroServe acquisitions brings our total to five transactions this year, expanding our capabilities and supporting long-term profitable growth. At the same time, we are investing in areas where we see the potential for compelling returns. That includes a $55 million investment in a Los Angeles site that will expand our Hub concept to the West Coast. These initiatives reflect our focus on deploying capital into markets where we see meaningful opportunities to grow.”
“We expect to enter 2027 with momentum across both operating segments,” Battles concluded. “Within Environmental Services, collection volumes into our disposal and recycling network remain strong, supported by improving U.S. manufacturing activity and continued reshoring. Our 10-year contract announced earlier this year has commenced in Q4, while our PFAS pipeline remains robust across both water filtration and remediation. We believe that recent large-scale emergency response projects and acquisitions further strengthen the outlook for Field Services, and our data center offerings are getting traction within Industrial Services. For SKSS, market conditions remain supportive of our re-refining spreads, complemented by solid execution across the business. Taken together, we see a clear path to achieve record results for 2026 and expect multiple catalysts for continued growth in 2027.”

Financial Guidance
In the fourth quarter of 2026, Clean Harbors expects Adjusted EBITDA to grow 23% to 26% year over year. Based on its year-to-date performance and current market conditions, Clean Harbors is raising the midpoint of both its 2026 Adjusted EBITDA guidance and adjusted free cash flow guidance by $80 million. For the full year, Clean Harbors now expects:
•Adjusted EBITDA in the range of $1.44 billion to $1.48 billion, with a midpoint of $1.46 billion. This Adjusted EBITDA range is based on anticipated GAAP net income in the range of $528 million to $563 million.
•Adjusted free cash flow in the range of $600 million to $660 million, with a midpoint of $630 million. This range is based on anticipated net cash from operating activities in the range of $970 million to $1,090 million.

Adjusted Free Cash Flow Reconciliation
Clean Harbors reports adjusted free cash flow, a non-GAAP measure, which it considers to be a measurement of liquidity that provides useful information to investors about its ability to generate cash. The Company defines adjusted free cash flow as net cash from operating activities less additions to property, plant and equipment plus proceeds from sale and disposal of fixed assets. When necessary, the Company adjusts for the cash impact of items derived from non-operating activities. Additionally, adjusted free cash flow excludes significant strategic growth investments, as they are not indicative of free cash flow for the current period. Adjusted free cash flow should not be considered an alternative to net cash from operating activities or other measurements under GAAP. Adjusted free cash flow is not calculated identically by all companies, and therefore the Company’s measurement of adjusted free cash flow may not be comparable to similarly titled measures reported by other companies.

About Clean Harbors
Clean Harbors (NYSE: CLH) is North America’s leading provider of environmental and industrial services. The Company serves a diverse customer base, including a majority of Fortune 500 companies. Its customer base spans a number of industries, including chemical, manufacturing and refining, as well as numerous government agencies. These customers rely on Clean Harbors to deliver a broad range of services such as end-to-end hazardous waste management, emergency spill response, industrial cleaning and maintenance, and recycling
