“We reported solid third quarter results supported by continued healthy portfolio performance, including stable credit quality and historically low levels of non-accruing loans and problem assets,” said Kort Schnabel, Chief Executive Officer of Ares Capital. “While a slower transaction environment led to a more measured pace of originations during the quarter, our investment backlog is up meaningfully from last quarter, and we are seeing stronger transaction activity entering the fourth quarter. We believe our market leadership, scale, diversified portfolio and strong financial position, along with the expected benefit from higher yields and our recently announced acquisition of MidCap Financial Investment Corporation, reinforce our strong underlying earnings power and our confidence in the current level of our regular quarterly dividend.”

“During the third quarter, we further strengthened our funding profile while maintaining significant financial flexibility,” said Scott Lem, Chief Financial Officer of Ares Capital. “We increased our debt capacity through a $750 million issuance of seven-year unsecured notes, while also reducing our funding costs through the reset of our debt securitization and modest utilization of our commercial paper program. With approximately $7 billion of available liquidity, prudent leverage and limited near-term maturities, our balance sheet remains well positioned to support our investment activity and future growth.”

In the third quarter of 2026, Ares Capital made new investment commitments of approximately $2.1 billion, of which approximately $1.6 billion were funded. New investment commitments were made to 25 new portfolio companies and 28 existing portfolio companies. As of September 30, 2026, 271 separate private equity sponsors were represented in Ares Capital’s portfolio. Of the approximately $2.1 billion in new commitments made during the third quarter of 2026, 71% were in first lien senior secured loans, 2% were in second lien senior secured loans, 7% were in senior subordinated loans, 18% were in Ares Capital’s investment in IHAM, 1% were in preferred equity and 1% were in other equity. Of the approximately $2.1 billion in new commitments, 85% were in floating rate debt securities, of which 74% contained interest rate floors. The weighted average yield of debt and other income producing securities funded during the period at amortized cost was 10.4% and the weighted average yield on total investments funded during the period at amortized cost was 10.2%. Ares Capital may seek to sell all or a portion of these new investment commitments, although there can be no assurance that Ares Capital will be able to do so. Also in the third quarter of 2026, Ares Capital funded approximately $711 million related to previously existing unfunded revolving and delayed draw loan commitments.

In the third quarter of 2026, Ares Capital exited approximately $2.5 billion of investment commitments, including approximately $1.2 billion of loans sold to IHAM or certain vehicles managed by IHAM. Of the approximately $2.5 billion of exited investment commitments, 83% were first lien senior secured loans, 10% were second lien senior secured loans, 6% were Ares Capital’s subordinated loan to IHAM and 1% were other equity. Of the approximately $2.5 billion of exited investment commitments, 95% were floating rate, 1% were fixed rate, 1% were non-income producing and 3% were on non-accrual status.
 
As of September 30, 2026 and December 31, 2025, the weighted average grade of the portfolio at fair value was 3.1 and 3.1, respectively, and loans on non-accrual status represented 2.4% of the total investments at amortized cost (or 1.4% at fair value) and 1.8% at amortized cost (or 1.2% at fair value), respectively. For more information on Ares Capital’s portfolio investment grades and loans on non-accrual status, see “Part I—Item 2. Management’s Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations—Portfolio and Investment Activity” in Ares Capital’s Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission (“SEC”) on October 27, 2026.

LIQUIDITY AND CAPITAL RESOURCES

As of September 30, 2026, Ares Capital had $722 million in cash and cash equivalents and $15.5 billion in total aggregate principal amount of debt outstanding ($15.1 billion at carrying value). Subject to borrowing base and other restrictions, Ares Capital had approximately $6.9 billion available for additional borrowings under its existing credit facilities as of September 30, 2026.

In July 2026, Ares Capital’s wholly-owned, consolidated subsidiary, Ares Direct Lending CLO 1 LLC (“ADL CLO 1”), completed a refinancing of its approximately $702 million term debt securitization with an approximately $709 million term debt securitization (as refinanced, the “ADL CLO 1 Debt Securitization”). The ADL CLO 1 Debt Securitization is also known as a collateralized loan obligation and is an on-balance-sheet financing incurred by Ares Capital.

The notes offered and term loans incurred in connection with the ADL CLO 1 Debt Securitization that mature on July 25, 2038 (collectively, the “July 2038 CLO Secured Debt”) were issued by ADL CLO 1 pursuant to the amended and restated indenture and security agreement governing the July 2038 CLO Notes (as defined below) and the Class A-1-LR credit agreement, as applicable, and include (i) $267.0 million of Class A-1-R Senior Floating Rate Notes, which bear interest at Term SOFR plus 1.46%; (ii) $24.5 million of Class A-2-R Senior Floating Rate Notes, which bear interest at Term SOFR plus 1.70%; (iii) $45.5 million of Class B-R Senior Floating Rate Notes, which bear interest at Term SOFR plus 1.90% (collectively, the “July 2038 CLO Notes”) and (iv) $139.0 million of Class A-1-LR term loans, which bear interest at Term SOFR plus 1.46%. In addition, ADL CLO issued an additional $7.1 million of subordinated notes (in addition to the $225.6 million of existing subordinated

notes previously issued by ADL CLO 1), which do not bear interest. Ares Capital retained all of the subordinated notes, which are unsecured obligations of ADL CLO 1 and are eliminated in consolidation. The proceeds from the ADL CLO 1 Debt Securitization were used in part to redeem all outstanding $406 million of Class A Senior Floating Rate Notes and $70 million of Class B Senior Floating Rate Notes issued by ADL CLO 1 in May 2024. In connection with the refinancing, Ares Capital realized a loss on extinguishment of debt of approximately $2 million. The refinancing effectively adjusted the interest rate charged on the July 2038 CLO Secured Debt from SOFR plus a blended weighted average spread of 1.86% to SOFR plus a blended weighted average spread of 1.51% and extended the stated maturity date from April 25, 2036 to July 25, 2038.

In July 2026, Ares Capital repaid in full the $1,000 million aggregate principal amount outstanding of its unsecured notes upon their maturity, which bore interest at a rate of 2.150% per annum.

In September 2026, Ares Capital issued $750 million in aggregate principal amount of unsecured notes, which bear interest at a rate of 6.250% per annum and mature on September 15, 2033 (the “September 2033 Notes”). The September 2033 Notes pay interest semi-annually and all principal is due upon maturity. The September 2033 Notes may be redeemed in whole or in part at any time at Ares Capital’s option at a redemption price equal to par plus a “make whole” premium, if applicable, as determined pursuant to the indenture governing the September 2033 Notes, and any accrued and unpaid interest. The September 2033 Notes were issued at a discount to the principal amount. In connection with the September 2033 Notes, Ares Capital entered into an interest rate swap for a total notional amount of $750 million that matures on September 15, 2033. Under the interest rate swap, Ares Capital will receive a fixed interest rate of 6.250% and pay a floating interest rate of three-month SOFR plus 1.8525%.

THIRD QUARTER 2026 DIVIDENDS PAID

On July 29, 2026, Ares Capital announced that its Board of Directors declared a third quarter 2026 dividend of $0.48 per share for a total of approximately $346 million. The third quarter 2026 dividend was paid on September 30, 2026 to stockholders of record as of September 15, 2026.

RECENT DEVELOPMENTS

From October 1, 2026 through October 22, 2026, Ares Capital made new investment commitments of approximately $1.1 billion, of which approximately $855 million were funded. Of the approximately $1.1 billion in new investment commitments, 90% were in first lien senior secured loans, 9% were in Ares Capital’s subordinated loan to IHAM and 1% were in preferred equity. Of the approximately $1.1 billion in new investment commitments, 88% were floating rate, 11% were fixed rate and 1% were non-income producing. The weighted average yield of debt and other income producing securities funded during the period at amortized cost was 10.0% and the weighted average yield on total investments funded during the period at amortized cost was 9.9%. Ares Capital may seek to sell all or a portion of these new investment commitments, although there can be no assurance that it will be able to do so.

From October 1, 2026 through October 22, 2026, Ares Capital exited approximately $727 million of investment commitments, including $497 million of loans sold to IHAM or certain vehicles managed by IHAM. Of the approximately $727 million of exited investment commitments, 93% were first lien senior secured loans, 1% were second lien senior secured loans and 6% were senior subordinated loans. Of the approximately $727 million of exited investment commitments, 94% were floating rate, 5% were fixed rate and 1% were on non-accrual status. The weighted average yield of debt and other income producing securities exited or repaid during the period at amortized cost was 9.2% and the weighted average yield on total investments exited or repaid during the period at amortized cost was 9.1%. Of the approximately $727 million of investment commitments exited from October 1, 2026 through October 22, 2026, Ares Capital recognized total net realized gains of approximately $2 million, including approximately $1 million of net realized losses recognized from the sale of loans to IHAM or certain vehicles managed by IHAM.

In addition, as of October 22, 2026, Ares Capital had an investment backlog of approximately $2.5 billion. Investment backlog includes transactions approved by Ares Capital’s investment adviser’s U.S. direct lending investment committee and/or for which a formal mandate, letter of intent or a signed commitment have been issued, and therefore Ares Capital believes are likely to close. The consummation of any of the investments in this backlog depends upon, among other things, one or more of the following: Ares Capital’s acceptance of the terms and structure of such investment and the execution and delivery of satisfactory transaction documentation. In addition, Ares Capital may sell all or a portion of these investments and certain of these investments may result in the repayment of existing investments. Ares Capital cannot assure you that it will make any of these investments or that Ares Capital will sell all or any portion of these investments.

ABOUT ARES CAPITAL CORPORATION
 
Founded in 2004, Ares Capital is a leading specialty finance company focused on providing direct loans and other investments in private middle market companies in the United States. Ares Capital’s objective is to source and invest in high-quality borrowers that need capital to achieve their business goals, which oftentimes can lead to economic growth and employment. Ares Capital believes its loans and other investments in these companies can help generate attractive levels of current income and potential capital appreciation for investors. Ares Capital, through its investment manager, utilizes its extensive, direct origination capabilities and incumbent borrower relationships to source and underwrite predominantly senior secured loans but also subordinated debt and equity investments. Ares Capital has elected to be regulated as a business development company (“BDC”) and was the largest publicly traded BDC by market capitalization as of September 30, 2026. Ares Capital is externally managed by a subsidiary of Ares Management Corporation (NYSE: ARES), a publicly traded, leading global alternative investment manager. For more information about Ares Capital, visit www.arescapitalcorp.com.
